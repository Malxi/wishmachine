# Setup / outline review checklist

Wishmachine's `check` and `audit` are deterministic. They can catch broken
structure and continuity, but they cannot judge whether a setting or plot is
good. Use this checklist after the deterministic checks pass.

## Setup review

- **Genre promise**: does the premise promise the genre the user asked for?
  A 修仙 story should not depend on spaceships, wormholes, or clone insurance
  unless the premise explicitly makes that crossover the point.
- **Causal spine**: write the premise as an `if -> then` chain. If the special
  rule is true, what follows for ordinary people, institutions and the
  protagonist? If the rule can be removed without changing the plot, it is
  decoration.
- **Cost and loophole**: every world rule needs a cost, a limit, and a way the
  protagonist can exploit it without breaking the rule.
- **Cast roles**: each core cast member needs a function, a relationship to the
  lead, a contradiction, and a reason to act before the plot needs them.
- **Resource scarcity**: who controls the scarce resource, who is excluded, and
  what does the protagonist trade to get it?
- **Theme**: does the premise make the theme arguable, rather than merely
  stating it?
- **Language and world**: check terms, titles, technology or cultivation
  vocabulary, anachronisms, and whether the world's rules are used
  consistently.

## Outline review

- **Global arc**: promise -> escalation -> midpoint reversal -> payoff ->
  cost. Each volume should change the protagonist's available choices.
- **Chapter function**: each chapter needs a goal, an obstacle, a turn, and a
  consequence. A chapter that only explains the setting is a setting note, not
  a chapter.
- **Event causality**: an event should be caused by a prior decision and force
  a later decision. Events that merely happen are filler.
- **Character agency**: the protagonist should make at least one consequential
  choice per chapter; other characters should want things that conflict.
- **Mystery discipline**: plant before resolve, resolve only what the reader
  could have noticed, and keep a cost for every answer.
- **Continuity**: time, location, resource ledgers, injuries, items and
  character presence must carry across chapters.
- **Pacing**: alternate pressure and breathing room; do not repeat the same
  event type or the same character tic until it becomes filler.
- **Expandability**: each outline chapter should contain enough concrete
  material for 3-5 prose scenes and the configured chapter length. If the
  outline summary is longer than the chapter prose, the chapter is compressed.

## Reporting

Report findings by severity:

```text
critical  premise or outline cannot support a story
high      contradiction, missing causal link, or broken promise
medium    weak motivation, repeated beat, or under-expanded chapter
low       polish, terminology, or optional texture
```

Do not claim a deterministic check proves literary quality. State clearly when
a finding is editorial judgment rather than a schema violation.
